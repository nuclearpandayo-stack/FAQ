Clan Rules

Our Rules

A few simple rules keep The Dark Omen a welcoming, active and enjoyable community for everyone.

☾
1
👥

Respect Everyone

This is an inclusive group where all types are accepted. No form of harassment will be tolerated, including sexual harassment, trolling, bullying, spamming of messages, racial, religious or political slurs, or any other mean-spirited behaviour.

Any incidents should be reported to an admin immediately.

2
🏰

Citadel Capping

Members are required to cap at our Citadel. If you do not cap for three weeks without a reason, you will be removed from the clan. You will receive a warning on the second week.

This ensures that we maintain an active clan with contributing members.

Being current with capping is also required for certain clan events, including giveaways.
3
🛡️

No Scamming or Luring

Scamming, luring or PKing another clan member is not permitted.

Advertising fake websites, phishing attempts or posting malicious links is also prohibited.

4
👑

Do Not Abuse Rank

Abusing the powers or privileges associated with your clan rank will result in an immediate demotion.

5
💬

Keep Drama Out of Clan Chat

If you have an issue with someone in the clan or Discord server, take the discussion to DMs or involve an admin.

Witnesses should not further escalate the situation. Screenshot the relevant conversation and send it to an admin so the matter can be resolved amicably.

6
🔇

Keep Language Reasonable

Excessive swearing or use of vulgar phrases is not allowed.

7
❤️

Respect Personal Boundaries

Conversations must stop immediately if a topic offends someone or makes another member uncomfortable.

Friendly banter is fine as long as it does not become excessive.

8
⚠️

Avoid Inflammatory Topics

Avoid discussions involving drugs, politics, gambling, religion, X-rated games and other potentially inflammatory or offensive subjects.

We are a diverse, worldwide clan and RuneScape is somewhere many members come to relax rather than debate divisive topics.

9
🎧

Bossing Voice Chat

Please use the bossing voice chat for teaching, learning and participating in PvM.

If you join to watch a stream and learn, remain muted where possible so players can clearly hear bossing instructions.

Other voice channels are available for general conversation.

10
🌐

Official Clan Language

The official clan language is English.

While we are an international clan, we cannot moderate conversations in every language. Full conversations should remain in English so that chat can be moderated effectively.

11
💬

Discord Is Part of the Clan Space

All clan rules also apply throughout our Discord server. Discord is considered an extension of our in-game clan space.

⚠️

If No Admin Is Online

If these rules are broken and there is no admin online, use the BLOCK feature where appropriate. Take screenshots of the relevant in-game or Discord conversation so the correct action can be taken.
