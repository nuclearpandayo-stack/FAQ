The Dark Omen Rules

Our rules exist to keep the clan welcoming, relaxed and enjoyable for everyone.

Before you start

The Dark Omen is an inclusive, international community. These rules apply both in-game and throughout our Discord server.

1

Respect Everyone

This is an inclusive group where all types are accepted. No form of harassment will be tolerated, including sexual harassment, trolling, bullying, message spamming, racial, religious or political slurs, or any other mean-spirited behaviour.

Any incidents should be reported to an admin immediately.

2

Citadel Capping

Members are required to cap at our Citadel.

If you do not cap for three weeks without a reason, you will be removed from the clan. You will receive a warning on the second week.

This helps ensure that the clan remains active and that members contribute to the Citadel.

🏰 Current capping is also a requirement for certain clan events, including giveaways.
3

No Scamming or Luring

Scamming, luring or PKing another clan member is not allowed.

Advertising fake websites, phishing attempts or posting malicious links is also prohibited.

4

Do Not Abuse Rank

Abusing the power or privileges of your clan rank will result in an immediate demotion.

5

Keep Drama Out of Clan Chat

If you have an issue with someone in the clan or Discord server, take the discussion to DMs or ask an admin to get involved.

If you witness an argument, please do not further escalate it. Take screenshots of the conversation and send them to a listed admin so the situation can be resolved appropriately.

6

Keep Language Reasonable

Excessive swearing or use of vulgar phrases is not allowed.

7

Respect Personal Boundaries

A conversation must stop if the topic offends someone or makes them uncomfortable.

Friendly banter is absolutely fine, as long as it does not become excessive.

8

Avoid Inflammatory Topics

Please avoid discussions involving drugs, politics, gambling, religion, X-rated games or other potentially offensive or inflammatory subjects.

We are a diverse and worldwide clan. RuneScape is somewhere many members come to relax and escape, rather than debate potentially divisive subjects.

9

Bossing Voice Chat

Please only use the bossing voice chat for teaching, learning and participating in PvM.

If you join to watch a stream and learn, please remain muted so everyone can clearly hear instructions during the boss encounter.

Other voice channels are available for general conversation.

10

Official Clan Language

The official clan language is English.

While we are an international clan, we cannot moderate conversations in every language. Please keep full conversations in English so that chat can be moderated effectively.

11

Discord Is Part of the Clan Space

All of these rules also apply to our Discord server. Discord is considered an extension of our in-game clan space.

⚠️ If something happens and no admin is online

If these rules are broken and there is no admin available to resolve the situation, please use the BLOCK feature where appropriate.

Take screenshots of the relevant in-game or Discord conversation and send them to a clan admin so the correct action can be taken.
