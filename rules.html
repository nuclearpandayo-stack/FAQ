The Dark Omen Rules

A few simple rules help keep the clan welcoming, respectful and enjoyable for everyone.

Community First

The Dark Omen is an inclusive, international clan. These rules apply both in-game and throughout our Discord server.

1

Respect Everyone

This is an inclusive group where all types are accepted. No form of harassment will be tolerated.

This includes sexual harassment, trolling, bullying, spamming, racial, religious or political slurs and other mean-spirited behaviour.

Any incidents should be reported to an admin immediately.

2

Citadel Capping

Members are required to cap at our Citadel.

If you do not cap for three weeks without reason, you will be removed from the clan. A warning will be issued on the second week.

🏰 Being current with capping is also required for certain events, including giveaways.
3

No Scamming or Luring

Scamming, luring or PKing another clan member is not permitted.

Fake websites, phishing attempts and malicious links are also prohibited.

4

Do Not Abuse Rank

Abusing the powers or privileges of your clan rank will result in immediate demotion.

5

Keep Drama Out of Clan Chat

If you have an issue with another clan or server member, take the discussion to DMs or involve an admin.

Witnesses should avoid escalating the situation. Screenshot the relevant chat and send it to an admin.

6

Keep Language Reasonable

Excessive swearing or use of vulgar phrases is not permitted.

7

Respect Personal Boundaries

Conversations should stop if the topic makes another member uncomfortable or causes offence.

Friendly banter is fine as long as it does not become excessive.

8

Avoid Inflammatory Topics

Avoid discussions around drugs, politics, gambling, religion, X-rated games and other potentially inflammatory subjects.

We are a diverse, worldwide clan and many people come to RuneScape to relax rather than debate.

9

Bossing Voice Chat

Bossing voice chat should primarily be used for teaching, learning and participating in PvM.

If you join to watch and learn, remain muted where possible so instructions can be heard clearly.

10

Official Clan Language

The official clan language is English.

Full conversations should remain in English so that moderators can properly monitor clan chat.

11

Discord Is Part of the Clan

All clan rules also apply throughout our Discord server. Discord is considered an extension of our in-game space.

⚠️ If No Admin Is Online

If a rule is broken and there is no admin available, use the BLOCK feature where appropriate.

Take screenshots of the relevant in-game or Discord conversation and send them to a clan admin so the correct action can be taken.
