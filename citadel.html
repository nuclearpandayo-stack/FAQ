01

Getting Started

Get to the Citadel

Travel to the Clan Citadel and enter through the portal.

02

Before You Start

Activate your capping boost

Once you're inside the Citadel, open the Citadel map and teleport to the town square.

⚡
Citadel Skilling Resource Boost

Select this before you start. It speeds up capping by 50%.

03

Find A Plot

Choose where you want to skill

Look to the right of your minimap and open the Citadel interface.

💡
You can move around

You don't need to stay at one plot. Switch between skilling areas whenever you want.

04

Weekly Progress

Fill your weekly resource cap

Start skilling at your chosen plot. As you gather resources, your weekly cap progress will increase.

✓
Cap complete

Once your Resource Cap is full, you're officially capped for the week.

05

Weekly Reward

Activate your XP boost

Return to the Avatar Control Stone and select Experience boost.

⚡
One week of bonus XP

Your XP boost lasts until the next weekly cycle.

06

Final Reward

Claim your bonus XP

Head into the Citadel Keep in the centre of the map.

🎁
Choose your skill

Pick which skill receives the bonus XP and claim your reward.

07

Finish Up

Let us know you've capped

If your RuneMetrics profile is public, your cap may be logged automatically.

💬
Not showing in the log?

Post in #citadel-cap so we know you've completed your weekly cap.

Why Cap?

What you get from capping

⚡

XP Boosts

Earn a weekly XP boost to help with training.

⭐

Clan Fealty

Increase your Fealty and improve Citadel XP rates.

🎁

Bonus XP

Claim additional XP from the Quartermaster.

🏰

Help the Clan

Your resources help maintain and improve the Citadel.
