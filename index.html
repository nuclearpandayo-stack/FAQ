Citadel Capping Guide

New to capping? No problem. Follow the steps below and you'll be done in no time.

The quick version

Go to the Citadel, activate your boost, skill until you hit your weekly cap, claim your rewards, and let us know you're done.

1

Get to the Citadel

Travel to the Clan Citadel and enter through the portal.

Head south-east from the Grand Exchange towards the Clan Camp.

2

Activate your capping boost

Once you're inside the Citadel, open the Citadel map and teleport to the town square.

Use the Citadel map to teleport straight to the town square.

At the town square, find the Avatar Control Stone. Right-click it and select Attune to Avatar.

Right-click the Avatar Control Stone and choose Attune to Avatar.

⚡ Select Citadel Skilling Resource Boost. This speeds up capping by 50%, so make sure it's active before you start!
3

Choose where you want to skill

Look to the right of your minimap and click the icon the arrow is pointing to below.

Click this icon to open the Citadel map.

You'll now see the Citadel map. The purple T icons are teleport points around the Citadel. Use these to quickly get to the skilling plot you want.

Click a teleport point to quickly travel around the Citadel.

💡 You don't have to stick to one plot. You can move between different skilling plots while working towards your weekly cap.
4

Fill your weekly resource cap

Once you've picked a plot, start skilling! As you gather resources, your progress towards your weekly cap will increase.

You can check your progress at any time in the Minigames interface. Keep an eye on the Resource Cap at the top.

Your Resource Cap shows how much you've collected this week.

🎉 Once your Resource Cap is full, you're officially capped for the week!
5

Activate your XP boost

Once you've finished capping, head back to the Avatar Control Stone from Step 2 and Attune to Avatar again.

This time, select Experience boost.

Choose Experience boost after you've finished capping.

⚡ Your XP boost lasts for one week — enjoy the extra XP!
6

Claim your bonus XP

There's one more reward to grab! Head into the Citadel Keep in the centre of the map.

Head into the Keep in the centre of the Citadel.

Inside, find the Quartermaster. Right-click them and select Claim XP. You'll then be able to choose which skill receives your bonus XP.

Right-click the Quartermaster and select Claim XP.

🎁 Pick your skill, claim your bonus XP, and you're done!
7

Let us know you've capped

If your RuneMetrics profile is public, your cap may be logged automatically.

If you don't appear in the log, post in #citadel-cap so we know you've completed it.

Why should I cap?

⚡ XP Boosts

Capping gives you an XP boost that can help while training throughout RuneScape.

⭐ Clan Fealty

Capping each week increases your Fealty, up to Rank 3, which improves your Citadel XP rates.

🎁 Bonus XP

You can claim additional XP from the Quartermaster after capping.

🏰 Help the Clan

The resources you gather help maintain and improve the Clan Citadel.
