Welcome to our community

The Dark Omen

The Dark Omen is an international RuneScape clan focused on creating a welcoming and enjoyable community for our members.

Whether you're interested in PvM, skilling, clan events or simply having people to chat with while you play, there's a place for you here.

This website contains useful information about the clan, our leadership, our rules and guides to help members get started.
